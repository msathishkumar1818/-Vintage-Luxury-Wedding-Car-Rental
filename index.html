The Vintage & Luxury Difference

Every Detail Curated.
Every Moment Remembered.

For over three decades, Vintage & Luxury Wedding Car Rental has been the standard-bearer of prestige bridal transport. Our collection is not simply a fleet — it is a living museum of automotive artistry, each motorcar personally selected, meticulously restored, and maintained to concours standards.

“We do not merely transport you. We compose the opening chapter of your love story.”

Precision is our silent vow. Two weeks prior to your celebration, our operations team conducts live route reconnaissance. On the wedding day, our senior chauffeur arrives a full 45 minutes ahead of schedule to ensure unhurried peace of mind.

“Punctuality in luxury is not a feature — it is a sacred ceremonial promise.”

From hand-tied Italian silk ribbons matched to your bridal gown to French crystal champagne flutes chilled in custom wood consoles, every sensory touchpoint is executed with dignified grace and tailored poise.

Bespoke Wedding Services

From Consultation
to Curtain Call

Your wedding day is choreographed with military precision and delivered with effortless grace. Our five-stage service protocol ensures that every transition — from the bridal home to the ceremony, to the reception and beyond — is seamless, punctual, and magnificently presented.

01

Private Consultation

A dedicated concierge meets you personally to understand your vision, palette, and timetable. We tailor every arrangement to your exact brief.

02

Fleet Selection & Viewing

Visit our private showroom to experience each motorcar in person. We hold a complimentary 30-minute private viewing for all confirmed bookings.

03

Bespoke Floral Dressing

Our in-house floral artisan dresses each motorcar with arrangements that complement your wedding flowers, colour scheme, and season.

04

Chauffeur Escort Service

Your personally assigned Master Chauffeur arrives 30 minutes early in full livery, coordinates with your wedding planner, and remains on-call throughout the day.

05

Grand Departure

We coordinate the perfect photographic moment and ensure your departure is an unforgettable scene — ribbons, confetti, and champagne included.
