Est. 1989

Three Decades of Royal Automotive Stewardship

Founded in 1989 by motoring historian and restoration specialist Edmund Hargrove, Vintage & Luxury Wedding Car Rental began as a single-motorcar private collection. Edmund's obsessive dedication to concours-standard restoration — and his belief that great motorcars deserve to be experienced, not merely preserved — gave birth to a company that now serves the most discerning couples across Britain and internationally.

Each motorcar in our collection is hand-selected after passing a rigorous three-year restoration programme overseen by our resident master coachbuilder. Every vehicle is maintained to its original manufacturer.
